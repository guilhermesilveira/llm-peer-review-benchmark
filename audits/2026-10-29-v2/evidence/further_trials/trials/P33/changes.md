# Paper P33 (DNL343 in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, one regimen of a multiregimen platform trial): published -> clean

- Design check: the primary (ECC) comparison uses only concurrently randomized placebo participants (63 randomized
  within the DNL343 regimen plus 76 from one concurrently enrolling regimen); there are no non-concurrent controls and
  all arms were randomized. The published Methods already analyse all randomized participants by randomized group with
  all available data, and report a sample size calculation.
- De-identification: platform trial name removed ("a multiregimen ALS platform trial", "a platform trial", "this
  trial"); regimen letter removed ("a regimen", "regimen-specific placebo" in Tables 1 and 2); trial network name and
  acronym removed ("74 centers of an academic ALS clinical trial network in the US", "this collaborative trial
  network"); sponsor name and location removed ("the manufacturer of DNL343 (the sponsor)"); name of the single review
  board removed (approval kept: "a single institutional review board"; "investigational" corrected to
  "institutional"); coordinating hospital removed ("a central coordination center"); assay vendor removed; the brand
  name of PB-TURSO in Table 1 replaced by the abbreviation used in the text; registration wording tidied. TRICALS risk
  score and El Escorial criteria names kept (established instruments). No author names in the converted text.
- Figures not reproduced; legends shortened. Participant flow from Figure 1 (taken from the figure description in the
  source XML; all counts add up) written into the Results: 117 of 711 master-protocol assessments not assigned to a
  regimen (98 did not meet criteria, 14 timed out of the screening window, 3 terminated early, 2 withdrew consent); 10 of
  the 259 assessments assigned to the DNL343 regimen not randomized (6 screen failures, 2 timed out, 1 terminated early,
  1 death); follow-up discontinued before week 24 by 30 DNL343 (26 early termination, 3 deaths, 1 withdrawn by the
  sponsor), 8 regimen-specific placebo (6, 2) and 13 shared placebo (10, 3) participants, so 156/186 (83.9%), 55/63
  (87.3%) and 126/139 (90.6%) completed the 24-week follow-up; total shared placebo group of 139 stated. The CSF GDF-15
  subset size (44 DNL343, 16 placebo) from Figure 3D added to the Results.
- ITT made explicit: "in accordance with the intention-to-treat principle" (Analysis Populations) and "All 325
  randomized participants initiated treatment and were included in the efficacy and safety analyses according to their
  randomized group, with all available data up to the end of follow-up" (Results).
- ALSFRS-R described in Outcomes as "a validated rating scale of bulbar, fine motor, gross motor, and respiratory
  function (total score range, 0-48, with higher scores indicating better function)" (basis for M4).
- Added to Results, consistent with Table 1 (basis for m3): "Bulbar-onset disease was present in 15.6% of the DNL343
  group and 12.2% of the shared placebo group, and the mean (SD) time since symptom onset was 19.7 (8.1) and 20.3 (7.6)
  months, respectively (Table 1)."
- Serious adverse events rephrased as a comparison sentence, same numbers (basis for m4): "were numerically more
  frequent in the DNL343 group than in the shared placebo group (28 participants [15.1%] vs 16 [11.5%])".
- Limitations: added the Hawthorne-type sentence "In addition, participants in both groups had frequent clinic or
  telephone visits and close monitoring throughout the trial; this attention, together with the awareness of taking
  part in a trial, may have influenced adherence, supportive care, and outcomes in both groups (a Hawthorne-type
  effect), so rates of functional decline outside a trial setting may differ."
- Internal inconsistencies corrected: (1) the pooled mean age of 59.3 years (abstract and Results) is impossible given
  Table 1 (59.5 years in 186 and 57.7 years in 139 participants; pooled 58.7); both places now give the group values
  from Table 1. (2) The NfL differences were given in "pg/mL" in the text but are log-scale differences (Table 2,
  log(pg/mL)); units corrected. (3) Table 2, ERO ALSFRS-R 95% CI "[2.31 to 0.48]" lacked the minus sign (estimate
  −0.92, SE 0.71); now "[−2.31 to 0.48]". (4) The Discussion called the NfL analysis "exploratory" and its P = .03
  result "not statistically significant"; now "Analysis of serum NfL, a secondary end point" and "not statistically
  significant within the prespecified testing hierarchy".
- Typographic slips corrected: "eIF2b" -> "eIF2B"; "phosphorylated eIF2B 2α" -> "phosphorylated eIF2α" (twice); "DNL43"
  -> "DNL343"; "change in baseline" -> "change from baseline"; "α =.05" and "P =." spacing; "PB TURSO" -> "PB-TURSO";
  table footnote superscripts removed and footnotes merged into the table notes; DNL343 n added to the Table 2 header.
- Left as published: 4 fatal TEAEs in the DNL343 group vs 3 deaths among those who discontinued follow-up before week
  24 (the fourth death can have occurred after week 24; safety follow-up ran to week 28).
- Text not shortened (5,689 words). No numerical result of the published article was changed apart from replacing the
  inconsistent pooled age by the Table 1 group values.
