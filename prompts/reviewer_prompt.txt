THE JOURNAL'S STANDARD GUIDANCE FOR REVIEWERS
Please comment on the following:
1. Originality: does the work add enough to what is already in the published literature? If so, what does it add?
2. Importance of the work to general readers: does it matter to clinicians, patients, teachers or policy makers?
3. Scientific reliability:
   - Research question: clearly defined and appropriately answered?
   - Overall design of study: adequate?

   - Methods: adequately described? Complies with the relevant reporting standard (e.g. CONSORT for randomised trials)? Ethical?

   - Interpretation and conclusions: warranted by and sufficiently derived from/focused on the data? Message clear?

5. State your recommendation: accept, minor revision, major revision, or reject.
